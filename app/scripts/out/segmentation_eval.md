# Online segmentation vs simulator truth

Generated by `app/scripts/evalSegmentation.ts` (n = 50 per speed × variant × platform cell, seed 7; 3000 normal trials per parameter set, plus 400 false-start, 100 no-move and 200 tremor × 2 trials). The same simulated trials are replayed for every parameter set. Errors are online − truth in ms (negative = the detector is early). Onset truth = movement start, end truth = phone reaches the nose. Every trial runs with resolveParams(base, condition), so the slow/fast/pause/jerky overrides of protocol.ts apply on top of each base set. Android-like trials are Chromium-quantised.

## False starts and no-move trials

false_start: the simulated participant twitches 800 ms after the trial starts (before the earliest possible GO with every set). "counted" = share of those twitches with timing.false_starts ≥ 1 (the controller cancelled the pending GO, played the error cue and restarted the hold-still); the rest were taken as still_lost or not noticed. The simulated twitch is deliberately large (≈ 3.8 m/s² step), so this checks the handling, not the sensitivity, of the false-start detector.
