\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the results mean.} The hand that brings a phone to the nose can be read from the phone's motion
sensors for persons the model has never seen. The signal is physical: early in the approach the phone turns about
gravity in opposite senses for the two hands, and only chiral quantities carry the hand. A 1D CNN on gravity- and
body-frame channels had the lowest log loss, although paired comparisons cannot separate it from the other leaders
(\R{final/concl_n_leaders} in all); the cross-lane selection that chose it in \R{final/sel_m3_deep} of
\R{final/sel_folds} folds has a selection-aware accuracy of \Rci{final/nested_accuracy}. Its body-frame input limits
the change on a grip absent from training to \Rb{final/final_grip_d}~points, against \Rb{final/rob_m2_lgbm_grip_d}
to \Rb{final/rob_m2_lr_grip_d}~points for the leaders on device-frame channels. With its in-fold Platt calibrator its percentage is
consistent with calibration, and combining three attempts with the scaled sum of logits raises the accuracy without
making the percentage over- or under-confident on average. The main risk lies before the
classifier: finding the approach without a cue costs accuracy, and natural free-choice movements are often
rejected.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item Only \R{describe/n_persons} persons, mostly students in their twenties; \R{describe/n_left_handed} left-handed and
    \R{describe/persons_ios} on iOS. Rare groups are barely represented.
  \item One free-choice trial per person, all right-hand: natural-use recall cannot show whether left-hand free
    choice is recognised as well, and a constant right answer would score 100\,\%.
  \item The main analysis uses the oracle windows stored by the app; the cue-free numbers come from lane m4's yaw8
    classifier, not the final model.
  \item Screen-away holds and multi-attempt sessions are synthesised from the recordings, not recorded.
  \item The final model's own LOSO numbers are optimistic: it was the best of \R{final/final_configs}
    configurations in its lane and was then chosen among \R{final/n_candidates} candidates on the outer folds, and
    its body-frame input was designed after outer-fold results had been seen. The nested estimate removes the
    optimism of choosing among the \R{final/n_candidates} candidates, not that of the lanes' own outer-fold choices
    (\R{final/n_exploratory_candidates} candidates; the CNN the best of \R{final/final_configs}) nor that of the
    post hoc body-frame design.
  \item The browser delivers about 60~Hz, and the app segmented slow trials with criteria that depend on the
    instructed speed, which affects every analysis stratified by speed (Appendix~\ref{app:sec:segmentation}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Next steps.}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item Run the final model inside the cue-free production simulation, and teach the detector free-choice approaches.
  \item Build the Stage~3 inference page from the exported model, with the rest-pose check, the stillness check and
    the displayed aggregate of equation~\eqref{eq:aggregate}.
  \item Record more participants, especially left-handed persons, and left-hand free-choice trials.
  \item Close the open reviews listed in the status note (Section~\ref{sec:intro}).
  \item Let the PI evaluate the page prospectively on new persons.
\end{enumerate}
