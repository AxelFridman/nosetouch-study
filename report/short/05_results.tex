\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

\paragraph{Model comparison.} Most candidates beat the reference floor yaw8 (\Rci{reference/accuracy}):
\R{final/paired_ref_better} of \R{final/paired_ref_m} are more accurate after Holm adjustment over these comparisons
(Figure~\ref{fig:FINAL_01_master_forest}). The best differ little: the candidate with the lowest log loss and those
that paired comparisons cannot separate from it (Holm over its \R{final/paired_best_m} comparisons) form
\R{final/concl_n_leaders} leaders, and since \R{final/n_exploratory_candidates} candidates were chosen on the outer
folds in their own lane, the comparison is exploratory.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\genfig{final/FINAL_01_master_forest}
\caption{The \R{final/n_candidates} candidates as their lanes deliver them (LOSO accuracy and log loss; dashed: the
reference floor; this lane: the final-model lane). The forest is exploratory; the nested estimate is the headline.}
\label{fig:FINAL_01_master_forest}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{The final model and its selection-aware estimate.} The final model is the body-frame 1D CNN, a
convolutional network on 12 gravity- and body-frame channels with the lowest log loss in production form. The
nested, selection-aware estimate, with the candidates fixed, is an accuracy of \Rci{final/nested_accuracy}, an AUC of
\Rcib{final/nested_auc} and a log loss of \Rcib{final/nested_log_loss}; inside the outer folds the selection chose the
CNN in \R{final/sel_m3_deep} of \R{final/sel_folds} (Figure~\ref{fig:FINAL_03_nested_selection}).

\paragraph{The shipped model's own numbers.} The shipped model's LOSO accuracy, \Rci{final/final_accuracy}, is
exploratory: it was the best of \R{final/final_configs} configurations in its lane, then chosen among
\R{final/n_candidates} candidates, an optimism of \Rcib{final/optimism_accuracy}~points over the nested estimate. Its
body-frame input was also designed after outer-fold results had been seen, an optimism no nested estimate removes,
and the rest of this section, which describes the shipped model, shares this status.

\begin{figure}[htbp]

folds (left), and the selection-aware estimate against the naive best pick (right).}
\label{fig:FINAL_03_nested_selection}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Robustness to an unseen grip.} Trained on upright grips only and tested on the sideways grips, the leaders
that read device-frame channels change by \Rb{final/rob_m2_lgbm_grip_d} to \Rb{final/rob_m2_lr_grip_d}~points (each
Holm $p \le$ \R{final/rob_m2_lgbm_grip_holm} over the \R{final/fig_grip_tested} grip tests;
Figure~\ref{fig:FINAL_06_robustness}). The body-frame CNN changes by \Rcib{final/final_grip_d}~points (unadjusted
$p$ = \R{final/final_grip_p}; Holm $p$ = \R{final/rob_m3_deep_grip_holm} over the same tests), and of the two leaders
without a Holm-significant drop the rule took the CNN as the more portable.

\paragraph{Screen facing away.} A phone held with the screen away defeats every model that reads the
screen-relative frame: on synthesised screen-away twins of the recorded trials the CNN falls to
\R{final/final_flip_raw}. A model-free check of the rest pose, exported with the model, and an un-flip recover
\R{final/final_flip_fixed}.

\paragraph{Calibration.} With its in-fold Platt calibrator~\cite{platt1999probabilistic}, the final model's
probabilities are consistent with calibration (Figure~\ref{fig:FINAL_08_calibration}): ECE \R{final/final_cal_ece},
inside its null band (up to \R{final/final_cal_ece_null_hi}), slope \Rcib{final/final_cal_slope} and intercept
\Rcib{final/final_cal_intercept}. No further remedy helps: Platt, temperature, isotonic or beta calibration on top
each raise the log loss slightly, none detectably (Holm $p$ = \R{final/cal_platt_holm} for each, over the four
remedies).

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\genfig{final/FINAL_08_calibration}
\caption{Reliability of the final model with its in-fold Platt calibrator, overall and per platform
(person-bootstrap 95\,\% bands). The percentage shown is consistent with calibration.}
\label{fig:FINAL_08_calibration}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Several attempts.} Combining attempts helps (Figure~\ref{fig:FINAL_10_attempts}): the mean \pright{} of
$k = 3$ attempts of one person and hand reaches an accuracy of \Rci{final/final_kthree}. The displayed aggregate of
equation~\eqref{eq:aggregate} is right in \R{final/agg_k3_sum_tau_acc} and neither over- nor under-confident on
average (mean confidence minus accuracy \Rcib{final/final_kthree_over}), a check of calibration in the large only.

The displayed scaled sum of logits (equation~\ref{eq:aggregate}) is neither over- nor under-confident on average.}
\label{fig:FINAL_10_attempts}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Who and where it fails.} The accuracy is \R{final/brk_android_acc} on Android and \R{final/brk_ios_acc}
on iOS (not compared by a test) and below \R{final/person_threshold} for \R{final/brk_below80} persons; the two
left-handed persons score \R{final/brk_left_p11} and \R{final/brk_left_p17} (Figure~\ref{fig:FINAL_11_persons}). Of
the \R{final/brk_err} errors, \R{final/brk_conf_err} are confident (a probability below 0.2 for the true hand), and
they cluster in a few persons (\R{final/brk_conf_p09} in P09, \R{final/brk_conf_p20} in P20).

\paragraph{Natural use.} On the free-choice trials the final model's right-hand recall is \R{final/final_nat_recall}
(\R{final/final_nat_credit} of \R{final/final_nat_n}), with no detectable shortfall against the same persons'
instructed trials (Holm $p$ = \R{final/nat_m3_deep_holm} over the \R{final/n_candidates} candidates). All free-choice
trials are right-hand trials, so a constant right answer would score 100\,\% and this recall says nothing about the
left hand.

\paragraph{More persons?} An inverse power law fitted to random subsets of training persons extrapolates the
accuracy to \Rci{final/lc_m3_deep_at40} at 40 persons, a band that understates the uncertainty. By this
extrapolation more persons of the same kind would add little average accuracy, so new persons are needed above all
for left-handers and left-hand free choice (full report, Appendix~\fullref{app:final}).
