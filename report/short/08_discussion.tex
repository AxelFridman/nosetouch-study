\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the results mean.} Early in the approach the phone turns about gravity in opposite senses for the
two hands, so the hand can be read from the motion sensors for persons the model has never seen: the cross-lane
selection that chose the body-frame 1D CNN has a selection-aware accuracy of \Rci{final/nested_accuracy}, the CNN's
body frame limits the change on an unseen grip to \Rb{final/final_grip_d}~points (device-frame leaders:
\Rb{final/rob_m2_lgbm_grip_d} to \Rb{final/rob_m2_lr_grip_d}), and with its in-fold Platt calibrator its percentage
is consistent with calibration. The main risk lies before the classifier: finding the approach without a cue costs
accuracy, and free-choice movements are often rejected.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item Only \R{describe/n_persons} persons, mostly students; \R{describe/n_left_handed} left-handed and
    \R{describe/persons_ios} on iOS.
  \item One free-choice trial per person, all right-hand: a constant right answer would score 100\,\%.
  \item The cue-free numbers come from lane m4's yaw8 classifier, not the final model.
  \item Screen-away holds and multi-attempt sessions are synthesised, not recorded.
  \item The shipped model's own numbers are optimistic: it was the best of \R{final/final_configs} configurations,
    then of \R{final/n_candidates} candidates, and its body-frame input was designed after outer-fold results had
    been seen. The nested estimate removes only the optimism of choosing among the \R{final/n_candidates}
    candidates, not that of the lanes' own outer-fold choices or of the post hoc body-frame design.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Next steps.}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item Run the final model in the cue-free simulation; teach the detector free-choice approaches.
  \item Build the inference page from the exported model, with the rest-pose check, the stillness check and the
    displayed aggregate of equation~\eqref{eq:aggregate}.
  \item Record more persons, especially left-handed persons, and left-hand free-choice trials.
  \item Let the PI evaluate the page prospectively on new persons.
\end{enumerate}
