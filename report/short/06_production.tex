\section{Towards production: no start cue}\label{sec:production}

\paragraph{Scope.} In production the page receives a continuous stream with no start cue: it must find the approach,
reject other movements, decide when to answer and show a calibrated \pright{}. Lane m4, the production simulation,
ran this pipeline on the full recordings with its reference classifier yaw8, \emph{not} the final model, to measure
what detection and stopping cost, so every cue-free number in this section is yaw8's; running the final model inside
it is the next step.

\paragraph{Finding the approach.} The baseline detector v0 takes the first movement after a fixed stillness: its
first detection is the approach in \R{describe/cuefree_share_approach} of the recordings and an earlier movement in
\R{describe/cuefree_share_earlier_movement} (Figure~\ref{fig:M4_01_detection_outcomes}). The production detector
scores candidate movements with a segment classifier that does not use the hand and accepts the most likely one when
its probability of being an approach is at least \R{m4/det_threshold}: its scored segment is the approach in
\R{m4/prod_share_approach} of the recordings and an earlier movement in \R{m4/prod_share_earlier_movement}, and it
rejects \R{m4/prod_share_rejected}.

\paragraph{What detection costs.} The classifier yaw8 reaches \Rci{m4/oracle_acc} on the stored windows and \Rci{m4/prod_acc} in
the production pipeline, where a rejection or an output of exactly 0.5 is an abstention with half credit. The
pipeline shows a hand for \R{derived/m4_prod_shown_coverage} of the recordings, right on
\R{derived/m4_prod_shown_acc} of those (forced choice \R{m4/prod_acc_forced}); detection changes the accuracy by
\Rcib{m4/d_prod_vs_oracle}~points (Holm $p$ = \R{m4/d_prod_vs_oracle_p_holm}, family of \R{m4/paired_family_n}
comparisons); the pipeline, best of \R{m4/n_configs_final}, is exploratory.

\paragraph{Stillness and screen away.} The detector relies on the stillness the app enforced: the pipeline falls
from \R{m4/stress_production_1_acc} with 1~s of stillness before the approach to \R{m4/stress_production_0_acc} with
none, when it answers only \R{m4/stress_production_0_cov} of the recordings. A model-free check of the rest pose
stops \R{m4/stressflip_production_rec_gate} of synthesised screen-away twins and \R{m4/stress_production_rec_gate}
of the recordings held toward the person.

\paragraph{Several attempts and the stopping rule.} On synthetic sessions spliced from each person's recordings,
combining up to three accepted attempts gives \Rci{m4/sess_1_k3_acc}, against \Rci{m4/sesstrue_1_k3_acc} with the
true approaches only, because only \R{m4/sessdet_precision} of the accepted attempts are approaches
(Figure~\ref{fig:M4_04_attempts}). The stopping rule answers once the aggregated \pright{} leaves the band between
$1 - p_{\mathrm{stop}}$ and $p_{\mathrm{stop}} = \R{m4/p_stop}$, or after \R{m4/k_max} attempts, and reaches
\Rci{m4/stop_1_acc} after \R{m4/stop_1_attempts} attempts on average; its scale was added after the held-out sessions
had been seen, so it is post hoc and exploratory (\R{m4/n_configs_sessions} configurations).

\paragraph{The calibrated output.} For one touch, the cue-free output is consistent with calibration: ECE
\R{m4/cal_nested_ece} (null band \R{m4/cal_nested_ece_null_lo} to \R{m4/cal_nested_ece_null_hi}), slope
\Rcib{m4/cal_nested_slope} and intercept \Rcib{m4/cal_nested_intercept}. At the stop the ECE of
\R{m4/stopcal_scale_sessions_ece} lies at the upper edge of its null band (up to
\R{m4/stopcal_scale_sessions_ece_null_hi}), a borderline result that needs a prospective test.

\paragraph{Natural use: the main failure mode.} The detector rejects \R{m4/nat_rejected} of \R{m4/nat_n}
free-choice recordings (\R{m4/nat_rejection_rate}, against \R{m4/prim_rejection_rate} on the primary set;
Poisson-binomial $p$ = \R{m4/nat_p_poisson}, unadjusted, a single pre-planned test). A hand is shown for
\R{derived/m4_prod_nat_n_shown} of the \R{m4/nat_n} (\R{m4/prod_nat_answered_tie} accepted recording got exactly
0.5), with a right-hand recall of \R{derived/m4_prod_nat_shown_acc}
(forced choice \R{m4/prod_nat_acc_forced}); all are right-hand trials, so a constant right answer would score
100\,\%.

\paragraph{For the user and the page.} Three instructions follow: hold the phone with the screen facing you, tilted
slightly up; keep still for 1~s before the movement; and make several attempts. The decision comes a median
\R{m4/lat_touch_ms}~ms after the screen first touches the nose; the exported final model (\R{final/export_params}
fitted numbers, \R{final/export_kb}~kB) reproduces the fitted pipeline's \pright{} to within \R{final/export_maxdp}
but has not yet run behind the cue-free detector (full report, Appendices~\fullref{app:prod:abstain}
and~\fullref{app:final:export}).
