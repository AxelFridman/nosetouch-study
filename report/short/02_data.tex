\section{Data}\label{sec:data}

\paragraph{App and protocol.} The data were recorded with NoseTouch, a web app that runs in the phone's browser and
guides the participant hands-free with spoken and visual prompts. Tier~0 of a visit is one free-choice trial, whose
hand the participant picks and reports afterwards (the natural-use set, scored but never trained on); tiers~1--4
prompt the hand, the grip and the speed in blocks of one hand (full report, Table~\fullref{tab:protocol}), and the
median visit took \R{describe/visit_minutes_median}~min.

\paragraph{Participants.} The participants were Bar-Ilan University students, most of them master's students, each
offered sweets for their time; a video shows a data-collection session\footnote{\raggedright A video of a
data-collection session:
\url{https://drive.google.com/file/d/1V9PF623HkJ8MjR6loJhUHnDkCx60W-Jl/view?usp=sharing}.}. One additional
participant, PD01, was analysed separately in an exploratory case study and never modelled
(Section~\ref{sec:secondary}).

\paragraph{Curation.} The data snapshot holds \R{describe/attempts_total} attempts, and a pre-specified exclusion
cascade keeps \R{describe/primary_trials} primary trials and \R{describe/natural_use_n} natural-use trials
(Figure~\ref{fig:S2_02_exclusion_flow}). Quality flags are kept and scored apart rather than excluded, so the models
see the data production will see, and labels are never changed.

\paragraph{The dataset.} The \R{describe/n_persons} persons are \R{describe/n_female} women and \R{describe/n_male}
men aged \R{describe/age_min} to \R{describe/age_max} years, \R{describe/n_left_handed} of them left-handed;
\R{describe/persons_android} recorded on Android and \R{describe/persons_ios} on iOS. The primary set has
\R{describe/primary_left} left-hand and \R{describe/primary_right} right-hand trials.

\paragraph{Gaps.} With \R{describe/n_left_handed} left-handed persons no effect of handedness can be estimated, and
with \R{describe/persons_ios} iPhone users platform comparisons have little power. All
\R{describe/natural_use_right} free-choice trials are right-hand trials, so natural use measures right-hand recall
only: a constant right answer would score 100\,\%.
