\section{Without a start cue}\label{sec:production}
In production the page receives a continuous stream: it must find the approach, reject other movements and decide
when to answer. A simulation on the \R{m4/n_recordings} full recordings (lane m4) ran this pipeline with its own
reference classifier, yaw8, so its numbers measure what detection costs, not how the final model performs without a
cue (full report, Appendix~\fullref{app:prod}).

The production detector cuts the stream into candidate movements and scores each with a classifier that does not use
the hand. The segment it hands to yaw8 is the approach in \R{m4/prod_share_approach} of the recordings, and it rejects
\R{m4/prod_share_rejected} (Figure~\ref{fig:M4_01_detection_outcomes}).

An abstention, a rejected recording or an output of exactly 0.5, is never a right-hand answer. The pipeline shows a
hand for \R{derived/m4_prod_shown_coverage} of the recordings and is right on \R{derived/m4_prod_shown_acc} of those
(forced choice \R{m4/prod_acc_forced}); it is exploratory, the best of \R{m4/n_configs_final} pipelines compared on
the outer folds.

Against yaw8 on the approach windows that the app stored during recording, detection changes the accuracy, with half
credit for abstentions, by \Rcib{m4/d_prod_vs_oracle}~points (Holm $p$ = \R{m4/d_prod_vs_oracle_p_holm} over
\R{m4/paired_family_n} comparisons).

In synthesised multi-attempt sessions only \R{m4/sessdet_precision} of the accepted attempts are approaches, and the
others cancel most of the gain of combining attempts. The stopping rule of Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline} is post hoc and
exploratory: its aggregation scale was added after the held-out sessions had shown this problem.

The detector rejects free-choice approaches more often than instructed ones (\R{m4/nat_rejected} of
\R{m4/nat_n}, \R{m4/nat_rejection_rate}, against \R{m4/prim_rejection_rate}; Poisson-binomial
$p$ = \R{m4/nat_p_poisson}, unadjusted, a single pre-planned test), so it must learn them before production.

Three instructions follow: hold the phone with the screen facing you, keep still for 1~s before the movement, and
make several attempts. The detector relies on that stillness: with none, the accuracy with half credit falls from
\R{m4/stress_production_1_acc} (1~s kept) to \R{m4/stress_production_0_acc}.
